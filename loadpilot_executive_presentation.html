Load it in reverse.
Drive it in order. Deliver it all.

LoadPilot is a dispatch co-pilot for primary and secondary distribution. Each morning it decides which trucks roll out and which corridor each driver takes. It then works out the drop order and where every carton sits, so drop 1 is at the door and the last drop sits behind the cab. Crews stop digging for cartons, and the fleet runs fewer kilometres.

How LoadPilot Works/One conversation · four optimisers

From order list to loaded, routed trucks in under a minute.

1

Ingest

Orders arrive as a list, a CSV/XLSX, a PDF or an email body. Cartons are captured from photos: QR codes and labels are read with Gemini vision.

2

Corridors & claims

Drivers claim a direction, e.g. “I've got West”. If several trucks share a corridor, LoadPilot splits it into branches.

3

Fleet & routes

An OR-Tools VRP handles a mixed fleet of 5 truck types, time windows and capacity. It can minimise cost, trucks or finish time.

4

LIFO loading

A 3D packer loads in reverse drop order, keeping fragile cartons on top and this-side-up cartons upright. It checks the door rule for every stop.

The door rule, visualised
T17-1 · 15 drops · S corridor · number = drop sequence

Loading follows the reverse of the route. At every stop, that stop's cartons are the first ones the crew reaches, with no re-handling. The same plan is shown in the agent as an animated 3D loading sequence (MP4 plus an interactive canvas).
